# Races
## Humans
Humans just like us, they live in ranches, towns and cities, hold festivals, have school, some technology and don't use any magic.
## Small people
Phisically look like humans, but they grow half the size, their cuisine is considered the best 
so many of them travel around to sell it at fairs and markets, their magic use is minimal. 
## Shark people
Native to the coast and islands, tall with shark heads and fins, they can travel the land thanks to the magical artifacts
they craft, which they also sell to the other races. They use magic for artifacts and shamanic purposes, plus thrive
as tour guides on their beautiful beaches. 
## Puma people
Descendants of mountain lions, they live a secluded life with a strict discipline code in the mountains. They do not use
magic as part of their principles. 
## Deer people
Tall people with deer heads, they study magic and are their main and most powerful users, but their very jealous about 
their knowledge. 
## Eagle people
Descendants of eagles, they live up in the canyon but don't form any kind of communities or towns, since they can just fly 
wherever they want when they need it.

# Other groups, creatures and organizations
## Human army
Organization of humans designated to protect them from any outside threats, they patrol and maintain order, they wear black,
the worst color to use under the sun, to show strenght and that they won't be intimidated by the weather.
